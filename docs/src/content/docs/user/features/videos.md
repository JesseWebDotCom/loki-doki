All your video sources in one app, played through your own server. Videos brings together **YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Reddit, and your own creations** in a single place, with one home feed, one library, one watch history, and one set of controls — no accounts, no cookies, and no "recommended for you" engineered to keep your kids glued to a screen.

## One app, many sources

The Home page interleaves what's popular and trending across every source you have enabled, along with a "Continue watching" shelf and uploads from creators you follow. Filter by source with one tap, or browse categories (Comedy, Sports, and so on) that mix results from everywhere. Each source also has its own space — YouTube with its full channel pages and Shorts view, TikTok and Vimeo browsing, Reddit's video communities — and an admin chooses which sources appear.

Two more "sources" round it out:

- **Mine** — your own videos: screen recordings, uploads, AI-generated clips, and anything you cut together in the built-in Create studio.
- **Clip a Link** — paste a video link from almost any website and Loki Doki resolves it, plays it, and can save it to your library.

## Watching

Videos play right in the app, streamed through your home server. While one plays you can:

- **Watch privately** — route the stream through your server so the platform never sees your browser (YouTube's "Private stream" mode; TikTok, Vimeo, and Reddit always play without an account).
- **Switch to audio mode** — play just the audio to save bandwidth, with the video's artwork as a backdrop and a live audio-reactive **EQ visualizer** dancing to the sound.
- **Minimize to the mini player** — keep watching in a docked corner player while you browse the rest of the app.
- **Pop out Picture-in-Picture** — float the video over other apps and windows.
- **Boost the volume** past 100% for quiet recordings, pick a playback speed, and choose a quality to suit your connection.
- **Skip the junk** — sponsor segments, intros, and self-promo skip automatically on YouTube, thanks to community-submitted markers.
- **Record live streams from the start** — a livestream can be captured as it happens and lands in your Offline library.

Chapters appear on the scrubber, comments and community ratings show where the platform has them, and the app quietly remembers where you stopped so every video resumes right where you left off — on any device.

## AI that works for you

Local AI cleans up what the platforms serve:

- **Smart titles** rewrite clickbait into a plain description of what the video actually is.
- **Smart descriptions** strip the promo codes, sponsor plugs, and link walls out of descriptions, leaving just the real notes ("View original" brings the raw text back).
- **AI summaries** give you the gist of a long video before you commit half an hour to it.
- **Transcripts** let you read along, search within a video, and jump to any line.

All of it runs on your own hardware — nothing is sent to a cloud AI.

## Following creators

Subscribe to YouTube channels, TikTok creators, Vimeo channels, and subreddits, and their newest uploads collect into one subscriptions feed — no algorithm reshuffling it. Every creator gets the same kind of page: banner, avatar, back catalogue, and playlists where the source has them. Add a creator by search or by pasting their link.

Already have a YouTube account? Link it with a TV-style code and your subscriptions, Watch Later, and Liked videos sync **both ways** — changes here push back to your account, and changes there flow in.

## Your library

- **Watch Later** and **Liked** work across every source and follow you to every device.
- **History** is one private, cross-source record of what you've watched, searchable and grouped by day.
- **Playlists** hold videos from any source side by side, with a queue view and autoplay.

## Offline

This is where Videos really earns its keep:

- **Save offline** — one click on any video (or just its audio) downloads it to your server, so it plays on a road trip or anywhere with no signal. Household members who save the same video share one copy.
- **Configure a creator for offline** — instead of saving videos one by one, set rules per creator: *keep the latest N videos offline automatically* and, optionally, *remove each one once you've watched it*. New uploads download themselves; old ones roll off. Your own manual saves are never touched by the rules.
- **Offline mode** — flip the app-wide Online/Offline toggle and Videos becomes a pure local library: everything saved plays instantly, everything else gracefully steps aside.
- **Enhance** — saved videos can be re-encoded in the background into a crisper rendition, per source, so low-quality sources look their best on a big screen.
- **Download to device** — pull a one-off copy of the video file (or audio) straight down to your phone or laptop, like any web download.

An admin can set per-source quality ceilings, and you can pick your own quality up to that limit.

## Plex

If your household runs Plex, every person can have their own **private Plex libraries of their saved videos — one per source** (YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Reddit, and your own creations). Saves appear in Plex automatically, sponsor segments can be cut from the exported files, and each library has its own policy: keep everything, keep only the most recent per creator, or delete a video from Plex once it's been watched — just like Plex's own downloads. See [Plex](../plex/) for setup.

## Turn videos into podcasts

Pick a video, a whole creator, or a playlist — from any source — and hand it to your AI hosts, who turn the content into a listenable podcast episode. Back on the watch page, a "Featured in podcasts" shelf links to episodes made from that video. See [Podcasts](../podcasts/) for how shows work.

## Create your own

The **Create** studio turns Videos into more than a viewer: record your screen or camera, upload clips, generate clips with AI, and cut them together into finished videos. Everything you make lives under **Mine**, plays like any other video, and can be saved to Plex or shared with the household.

## Privacy in plain terms

Everything runs through your home server: the video streams, the thumbnails and avatars, the subscription feeds, even the sponsor-skip lookups. The platforms never learn who's watching, and nobody in your house is being profiled. It's the parts of these services you want, without the parts you don't.
