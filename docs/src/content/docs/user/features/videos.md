**One place for all your video stuff.** Videos brings YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Reddit, and your own creations together in a single app played through your own server - one home feed, one search, one library, one watch history, one set of subscriptions, and one set of controls, instead of five apps each fighting for your attention. No accounts, no cookies, no "recommended for you" engineered to keep your kids glued to a screen - and none of the constant selling that comes with modern video.

## One app, many sources

The Home page interleaves what's popular and trending across every source you have enabled, along with a "Continue watching" shelf and uploads from creators you follow. Filter by source with one tap, switch between card, list, and large views, or browse categories (Comedy, Sports, and so on) that mix results from everywhere. Each source also has its own space - YouTube with its full channel pages, topic shelves, and a swipeable vertical Shorts feed; TikTok and Vimeo browsing; Reddit's video communities - and an admin chooses which sources appear.

Two more "sources" round it out:

- **Mine** - your own videos: screen recordings, uploads, AI-generated clips, and anything you cut together in the built-in Create studio.
- **Clip a Link** - paste a video link from almost any website and Loki Doki resolves it, plays it, and can save the video (or just its audio) to your library.

## Search

One search box covers every source at once: results from YouTube, Reddit, and Vimeo interleave on a single page, with chips to narrow to one source. Paste a video or creator URL instead of a query and you jump straight to it. Videos also show up in the app-wide search (Cmd+K), and you can ask your companion to play something by name.

## Watching

Videos play right in the app, streamed through your home server. While one plays you can:

- **Watch privately** - route the stream through your server so the platform never sees your browser (YouTube's "Private stream" mode; TikTok, Vimeo, and Reddit always play without an account).
- **Switch to audio mode** - play just the audio to save bandwidth, with the video's artwork as a backdrop and a live audio-reactive **EQ visualizer** dancing to the sound.
- **Minimize to the mini player** - keep watching in a docked corner player while you browse the rest of the app; it follows you everywhere and can advance through a queue.
- **Pop out Picture-in-Picture** - float the video over other apps and windows.
- **Take control of playback** - speed from 0.25× to 2×, a quality picker, a **volume boost** up to 4× for quiet recordings, fullscreen with a zoom-to-fill option, and frame previews when you hover the scrubber.
- **Skip the junk** - sponsor reads, intros, and self-promo skip automatically (see "The end of the constant selling" below).
- **Record live streams from the start** - a livestream can be captured as it happens and lands in your Offline library.

Chapters appear on the scrubber, comments and restored like/dislike counts show where the platform has them, cards play a silent preview when you hover them, and an **Up next** column with autoplay (and a cancellable countdown) keeps a session going. The app quietly remembers where you stopped, so every video resumes right where you left off - on any device.

## The end of the constant selling

Modern video is one long pitch: clickbait to get the click, a sponsor read, "smash that subscribe," a giveaway, and a description that's all product links and promo codes. Videos strips that layer off:

- **Anti-clickbait** - community-voted titles and thumbnails replace YouTube's clickbait with a plain description of what the video actually is (optional, one toggle).
- **Sponsor reads, subscribe-begging, and self-promo skip themselves** during playback, using community-submitted markers - you pick which categories to skip.
- **AI descriptions** clean the spam out: sponsor plugs, product links, promo codes, and giveaway pitches are stripped from descriptions, leaving just the real notes - and where a video has no useful description, the AI fills in the gap. "View original" brings the raw text back anytime.
- **Smart titles** - where a source gives videos no real titles at all (TikTok), local AI writes one from the video's captions.
- **No ads** - streaming through your own server means no pre-roll, no banners, no shopping shelf under the player.

And local AI helps you decide what's worth your time:

- **AI summaries** give you the gist of a long video before you commit half an hour to it.
- **Transcripts** let you read along, search within a video, highlight the current line as it plays, and jump anywhere with a click.

The AI runs on your own hardware - nothing is sent to a cloud service.

## Following creators

Subscribe to YouTube channels, TikTok creators, Vimeo channels, and subreddits, and their newest uploads collect into one subscriptions feed - no algorithm reshuffling it. Every creator gets the same kind of page: banner, avatar, back catalogue in tabs (Videos, Shorts, Live, Playlists where the source has them), and an about section with links. Add a creator by search or by pasting their link.

Bringing an existing YouTube life with you is easy:

- **Import your subscriptions** from a Google Takeout CSV in one go.
- **Link your YouTube account** with a TV-style code and your subscriptions, Watch Later, and Liked videos sync **both ways** - changes here push back to your account, and changes there flow in.

Creators can also work for you automatically: besides the offline rules below, a creator or playlist can **auto-generate podcast episodes** from each new upload, and a single master switch pauses all automation when you want quiet.

## Your library

- **Watch Later** and **Liked** work across every source and follow you to every device.
- **History** is one private, cross-source record of what you've watched - searchable, grouped by day, with per-item removal or a full clear.
- **Playlists** hold videos from any source side by side. Play all or shuffle, drag to reorder, download the whole list for offline in one go, **share a playlist with the family**, and clone someone else's shared playlist into your own library.

## Offline

This is where Videos really earns its keep:

- **Save offline** - one click on any video (or just its audio) downloads it to your server, so it plays on a road trip or anywhere with no signal. Household members who save the same video share one copy.
- **Configure a creator for offline** - instead of saving videos one by one, set rules per creator: *keep the latest N videos offline automatically* and, optionally, *remove each one once you've watched it*. New uploads download themselves; old ones roll off. Your own manual saves are never touched by the rules.
- **Offline mode** - flip the app-wide Online/Offline toggle and Videos becomes a pure local library: everything saved plays instantly, everything else gracefully steps aside.
- **Enhance** - saved videos can be denoised and sharpened in the background into a crisper rendition, per source, so low-quality sources look their best on a big screen.
- **Download to device** - pull a one-off copy of the video file (or audio) straight down to your phone or laptop, like any web download.

The Offline page shows everything saved across all sources with live download progress, cancel buttons, and bulk select for cleaning up. An admin can set per-source quality ceilings, and you pick your own quality up to that limit.

## Plex

If your household runs Plex, every person can have their own **private Plex libraries of their saved videos - one per source** (YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Reddit, and your own creations). Saves appear in Plex automatically, sponsor segments can be cut from the exported files, and each library has its own policy: keep everything, keep only the most recent per creator, or delete a video from Plex once it's been watched - just like Plex's own downloads. See [Plex](../plex/) for setup.

## Turn videos into podcasts

Pick a video, a whole creator, or a playlist - from any source - and hand it to your AI hosts, who turn the content into a listenable podcast episode; creators you follow can even feed a show automatically as they upload. Back on the watch page, a "Featured in podcasts" shelf links to episodes made from that video. See [Podcasts](../podcasts/) for how shows work.

## Create your own

The **Create** studio turns Videos into more than a viewer:

- **Generate clips with AI** - text-to-video or image-to-video, with style models to pick from and live progress as frames render.
- **Edit in a real timeline** - a media bin, preview player, timeline with undo/redo and autosave, and an export dialog that renders a finished video.
- Everything you make lives under **Mine**, plays like any other video, joins playlists and Watch Later, and can be **shared with the household** - shared videos even land in the other person's Plex library.

## Privacy in plain terms

Everything runs through your home server: the video streams, the thumbnails and avatars, the subscription feeds, even the sponsor-skip lookups. The platforms never learn who's watching, nobody in your house is being profiled, and household content-profile rules apply here like everywhere else in Loki Doki. It's the parts of these services you want, without the parts you don't.
