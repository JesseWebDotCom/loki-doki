The Music app makes short instrumental tracks on the spot, with no internet, no cloud service, and no AI model to download. Everything is generated right in your browser, so it works the same on a plane as it does at home. Pick a vibe, press Generate, and you get real, listenable music.

## Generate

Choose a style, like Warm, Lo-fi, Cinematic, Synthwave, Funk, or Ambient, and pick what kind of track you want:

- **Full track**: a longer stand-alone piece that builds in and rings out.
- **Loop / bed**: a seamless piece you can loop under something else.
- **Intro** or **Outro**: short stings, the same kind used for podcast shows.

Press Generate and you get six different takes to listen through. Each one is a genuinely different arrangement, not the same loop, so you can pick the one you like. Press Regenerate for six fresh takes. You can also nudge the tempo and key if you want a specific feel, and for full tracks and loops you can choose how many bars long they are.

When you find a take you like, give it a name and save it to your Library.

## Remix

Have a MIDI file? Drop it in and the Remix tab restyles it. It keeps the actual notes from your file, the melody, bass, and chords are still the composer's own, and re-points them to a chosen style's instruments with a fresh beat underneath. The result sounds like your song in a new outfit, not a different song. As with Generate, you get several takes to choose from, then save the one you want.

## Library

Everything you save lands in your Library, where you can play it back, rename it, or delete it. Your tracks are private to you.

## Fully Offline, Always

There is no streaming service behind this and nothing leaves your home. The only thing the app fetches is a small instrument sound bank the first time you use it, and after that even that is cached. From then on, making music works completely offline.
