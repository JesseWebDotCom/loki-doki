Your companion is a friendly AI personality that lives in a little floating bar at the bottom of the screen. It has a name, a personality, a voice, and an animated avatar that breathes, reacts, and moves its mouth as it talks.

And it belongs to you. It isn't built to hook you or upsell you, there's no subscription keeping it "alive," and it can't be quietly updated into something creepy. You set the name, personality, voice, and look, and it stays exactly that way on your own hardware.

## Meet Your Companion

Loki Doki ships with a roster of ready-made companions so there's always someone to talk to, including everyday buddies, mentors and coaches, kid-friendly storytellers and study helpers, calm wellness listeners, and creative muses. Pick one and start chatting right away.

## Browse the Companion Store

Open the **Companion Store** at **/companions** to see everyone, App-Store style:

- **Categories** group companions by what they're best at: Everyday & Assistants, Coaches & Mentors, Family & Kids, Wellness, Creative, and a grown-up Mature (18+) tier.
- Each companion has its own page with a short bio, its voice, speaking pace, reply style, and full personality description, plus a **Select** button to make it your active companion and a **Preview** button to hear its voice.
- Some Mature companions appear **Locked** until your content settings allow them. You can raise your content ceiling in Settings to unlock them (your admin may also limit this).

## Favorite and Switch

- Tap the **heart** on any companion to add it to your **Favorites** for quick switching.
- Your favorites show up right in the companion menu, so you can hop between, say, a kids' storyteller and a study coach in one tap.
- Click the avatar in the floating bar to open the companion menu, switch companions, or turn the companion off entirely.

## Talking to Your Companion

- Type in the floating input bar for a quick, casual conversation. Press **⌘ /** (or **Ctrl /**) from anywhere to jump straight to the input.
- Your companion also joins the main chat.
- If voice is enabled, you can turn on **read-aloud** and **hands-free** listening, and say your companion's wake word to talk to it directly. See [Voice](/user/features/voice/) for setup.
- The bar comes in three sizes, Mini, Docked, and Max, so you can keep it tiny or give it room. After a few minutes of quiet, your companion gently dozes off until you come back.

## Expressions and Animations

Companions react to the conversation:

- **Breathing animation** while idle
- **Head tilt** when thinking, listening, or asking a question
- **Moods** that animate the eyes and brows
- **Lip sync** so the mouth moves in time with speech (in step with the spoken audio when voice is on, or with the words as they appear when it's off)

## Make It Yours

Admins can fully customize companions in **Admin → Companions → Characters**: change the name, rewrite the personality, pick the DiceBear avatar look, assign a voice and speaking pace, set a wake word, and choose a category. You can build a brand-new companion from scratch and test it live before saving. Nothing about your companion is rented or remote; it's yours, running at home.
