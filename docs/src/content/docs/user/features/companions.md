Your companion is fully customizable and comes to life as an animated avatar: it breathes, reacts, and lip-syncs as it speaks. It also belongs to you. It isn't built to hook you or upsell you, there's no subscription keeping it "alive," and it can't be updated into something creepy. You set the name, personality, voice, and look, and it stays exactly that way on your hardware.

## What Is a Companion?

A companion is an AI personality with its own name, personality, voice, and avatar. They appear as a floating overlay in the app and can be interacted with directly.

## Choosing a Companion

Open your profile and select from the available companions. Your admin may have set up multiple companions with different personalities and voices.

## Interacting with Your Companion

- Type in the companion's floating input field for a direct, casual conversation
- The companion also participates in the main chat
- Say the companion's wakeword (if set) to activate voice interaction specifically with that companion

## Expressions and Animations

Companions react to the conversation:
- **Breathing animation**: subtle idle breathing
- **Head tilt**: when thinking or asking a question
- **Startle**: when surprised or corrected
- **Lip sync**: mouth moves in time with speech

## Companion Voices

Each companion has a distinct voice. You can hear a preview of any voice in your profile settings (if your admin has enabled this).
