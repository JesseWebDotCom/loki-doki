Wakeword detection, speech-to-text, and the spoken reply all run on your own machine. Your voice is never streamed to a cloud service.

## How It Works

Say your wakeword (configured by your admin) and the app starts listening. Speak your message, and the AI responds in the companion's voice.

## Wakeword

The wakeword is a specific phrase that activates the microphone. You can see which wakeword is active in your profile settings.

Say the wakeword again during a spoken response to interrupt and ask a new question.

## Voice Settings

In your profile, you can:
- Choose the companion voice
- Adjust TTS speaking speed
- Enable/disable wakeword detection

## Tips

- Speak clearly and at a normal pace; Whisper handles accents and varied speech well
- You don't need to raise your voice; normal conversational volume works
- If the wakeword isn't responding, check that your browser has microphone permission
