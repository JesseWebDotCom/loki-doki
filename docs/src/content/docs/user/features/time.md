Time is a simple, private clock app: see the time around the world, set alarms and timers, and run a stopwatch. When an alarm or timer goes off, your companion can announce it out loud.

## World clock

Add the cities you care about and see them all at once, with the current time and day for each. Handy for family or coworkers in other time zones.

## Alarms

Set one-off or recurring alarms with the days they should repeat. When an alarm fires, you get an on-screen prompt and a tone, and your companion can speak it aloud — with **snooze** and **dismiss** right there. Alarm tones use the same offline music engine as the Music app, so you can pick a sound without downloading anything.

## Timers

Start one or more countdown timers (great for cooking or focus sessions). They keep running as you move around the app, and announce themselves when they finish.

## Stopwatch

A straightforward stopwatch with laps, for timing anything on the spot.

## Your privacy

Your clocks, alarms, and timers are saved to your own account on your server. Nothing leaves your home.
