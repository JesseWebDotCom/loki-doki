Watch YouTube without the tracking or the rabbit hole that comes with it. Loki Doki plays videos through your own server, so the browser never talks to Google. No account, no cookies, and no "recommended for you" engineered to keep your kids glued to the screen.

## Watching a Video

Search for anything, or open a video from your subscriptions, and it plays right in the app. Because the video streams through your home server, nothing reports back about what you watched.

While a video plays, you can:

- Pick a quality (auto, 720p, or 360p) to suit your connection.
- See the channel, description, and a transcript you can read along with.
- Get an AI-written summary of long videos, so you can decide if they are worth your time.
- Skip sponsor segments, intros, and self-promo automatically, thanks to community-submitted skip markers.

The app quietly remembers where you stopped, so you can pick a video back up later right where you left off.

## Following Channels

Subscribe to channels you like and their newest uploads collect into one feed, the way a subscriptions page should work, with no algorithm reshuffling it. You can add a channel by pasting its link or handle, and if you already have a YouTube account you can import your whole subscription list from a Google Takeout CSV file.

Each channel also has its own page where you can browse its full back catalogue, not just the last handful of uploads.

## Finding New Things

The Home page surfaces what is popular and trending, plus a "Recommended for you" shelf that learns from what you have actually watched, not from a profile sold to advertisers. There is also a Shorts view for quick clips, and you can browse playlists you find in search.

## Watch Later, Liked, and History

Save a video to **Watch Later** or mark it **Liked**, and those lists follow you across every device. Your **History** keeps a private record of what you have watched so you can find that video again later.

## Save It for Offline

Save any video, or just its audio, to your own library so it plays even when you are off the internet, on a road trip or somewhere with no signal. An admin can set a maximum save quality, and you can choose your own quality up to that ceiling. You can also download a one-off copy of a video or its audio straight to your device.

## Turn Videos into a Podcast

Pick a video, a whole channel, or a playlist and Loki Doki can hand it to your AI hosts, who turn the content into a listenable podcast episode you can play later. See the [Podcasts](/user/features/podcasts/) page for how that works.

## Privacy in Plain Terms

Everything in YouTube runs through your home server: the video stream, the thumbnails, the subscription feeds, even the sponsor-skip lookups. Google never learns who is watching, and your kids are not being profiled. It is the parts of YouTube you want, without the parts you do not.
