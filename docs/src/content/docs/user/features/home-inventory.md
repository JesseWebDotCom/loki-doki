Your home's details (what you own, where it is, what it's worth) stay on your hardware. The AI identifies devices and keeps the records here, not in a vendor's cloud.

## Overview

Home Inventory is an AI-powered tracker for your home's appliances and devices. Take a photo of any device and the AI identifies it, looks up its specs, and creates a record automatically.

## Adding a Device

1. Open Home Inventory (`/home-inventory`)
2. Tap **Add Item**
3. Take a photo or upload one
4. The AI identifies the device and fills in the details: make, model, serial number, and warranty period
5. Review and save

## Service Log

Each item has a service log where you can record:
- Repairs and maintenance
- Cost and technician
- Photos (before and after)
- Next service reminder

## Warranty Alerts

Items with known warranty expiry dates show alerts on the Today page as the date approaches. Add the purchase date and warranty period when creating an item to enable this.

## Asking the AI

You can ask the AI about your inventory in chat:
- _"What appliances are in the garage?"_
- _"When was the HVAC last serviced?"_
- _"Find the manual for my dishwasher"_

## Manuals

If your admin has enabled manual caching, you can access offline-cached PDF manuals for your devices and ask the AI questions about them.
