Maps, search, and routing all run on your own server. Your location and the places you look up never touch an online service, and the whole thing keeps working when the internet doesn't.

## Overview

The Maps feature gives you fully offline maps and navigation for the regions your admin has set up. No tile API, no data plan, no internet required.

## Finding a Place

Type a location, address, or place name into the search bar. Results come from a local geocoder index. They are not sent to any online service.

## Getting Directions

Tap a location on the map and choose **Directions**. Select your travel mode (walking, driving, or cycling) and the route is calculated locally via GraphHopper.

## Available Regions

The regions available depend on what your admin has downloaded. Common setups include:
- Local city or region
- State or country extract
- Custom bounding box

If your area isn't covered, ask your admin to download the relevant region.

## Offline Navigation

Once a route is calculated, the full turn-by-turn list is available. The map works completely offline. No internet is needed once the tiles are downloaded.
