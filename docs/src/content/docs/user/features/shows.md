Shows helps you find your next series and keep track of what you're watching. Search any TV show to see where it streams, watch trailers, read a synopsis and reviews, and add it to a personal watchlist — all without a Netflix-style algorithm deciding for you.

## Finding a show

Search by title, or browse what's popular and trending. Open any show to get:

- **Where to Watch** — the streaming services (and regions) carrying it, so you know exactly where to go.
- **Trailers &amp; clips** — official trailers and previews.
- **About** — synopsis, cast, ratings, and a parents guide.
- **Reviews** — what critics and audiences thought.

## Your watchlist &amp; progress

Add shows to your **watchlist** to keep them handy, and track your **progress** as you watch. Your list is private to your account.

## On your Plex

If your household runs a **Plex** server, Shows marks the titles you already own with a **Plex badge**, so you can tell at a glance what's available at home versus what you'd need to stream.

## Listen to it as a podcast

Turn a show into an **AI-generated podcast** — a spoken rundown you can listen to, generated per episode or as a season overview, in voices you choose.

## Your privacy

Everything is fetched by your own server from open sources. Your watchlist and viewing progress stay in your account and never leave your home.
