A storefront for ebooks and audiobooks, with your own private library, no account or subscription required.

## Overview

Books shelves anything you'd actually read cover to cover: novels, classics, textbooks, manuals, comics, and magazines. Looking for a dictionary, an encyclopedia, or a quick lookup instead? That's [Reference](../reference/).

## Where books come from

Browse and search across several sources at once:

- **Project Gutenberg** and **Standard Ebooks** for public-domain classics
- **Internet Archive** and **LibriVox** for audiobooks
- **Wikisource** and other reference-style book packs
- Any **self-hosted library** your admin connects (Calibre-Web, Kavita, and similar)
- Your own **uploads** (EPUB, PDF, CBZ/CBR comics)

## Save vs. Download

A book can be in your library two ways:

- **Save** just remembers you're interested, no file is downloaded yet.
- **Download** pulls the actual book (or audiobook) onto your server so it opens instantly and works with no internet at all.

You can switch a saved title to a full download at any time from its detail page.

## Reading and Listening

Ebooks open in a clean, distraction-free reader with adjustable text size and your progress saved automatically. Comics get a dedicated page-by-page viewer. Audiobooks play with chapters, a normal scrub bar, and resume exactly where you left off.

Don't have narrated audio for something? Ask for **text-to-speech** and Books will read it aloud in a voice you choose, turning any ebook into an audiobook.

## Magazines

Browse current and back issues across a set of built-in categories, from tech to gaming, read straight in the app.

## Write Your Own Book

Books includes an AI authoring tool: describe a premise, genre, and length, and your companion drafts an original book with you, one step at a time:

1. It proposes a **story bible**, characters, setting, tone, and themes, for you to review and edit.
2. It writes a **sample chapter** so you can confirm the voice feels right before committing to the whole thing.
3. It generates the rest **chapter by chapter**, keeping track of continuity as the story goes.

You can also point it at a book already in your library and ask it to **continue** the story or **reshape** it (a different ending, a new POV, and so on). Nothing is added to your library until you approve the finished draft.

## Admin

Your admin can add self-hosted library sources and API keys for extra metadata lookups under Admin → Integrations → Books.
