If your household runs a [Plex](https://www.plex.tv) media server, Loki Doki connects to it so the **Shows** and **Movies** apps know what's already in your library. No more paying to stream something you already own.

## What it adds

- **Plex badge** — when you look up a show or movie, Loki Doki checks your Plex library and marks the titles you already have, so you can tell at a glance what's at home versus what needs streaming.
- **Recently added** — see what's new on your server.
- **Add to Plex watchlist** — push a title you find in Loki Doki straight to your Plex watchlist.

## Setting it up

An admin connects the Plex server (URL and access token) in the admin settings. Once linked, the badges and library checks appear automatically across Shows and Movies. The connection is read-mostly — Loki Doki looks up what you own and can add to your watchlist, nothing more.

## Your privacy

The link to your Plex server stays on your own network. Library lookups happen between your Loki Doki instance and your Plex server directly; nothing about your collection is sent to a third party.
