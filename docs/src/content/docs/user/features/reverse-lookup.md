Reverse Lookup answers "who lives there?" and "who called me?" using public records, right from your own app.

## Three Ways to Search

Switch between three tabs depending on what you're starting from:

- **Address**: enter an address and get back the property record (owner, bed/bath count, square footage, year built, lot size, style, appraised and land value, last sale price and date, zoning, and notable features, with a link to the full assessor record) plus a list of people known to live there.
- **Name**: search a person's name to get back their known phone numbers (tap to call), up to three associated addresses, and known associates.
- **Phone**: search a phone number to identify who it belongs to, with the same address and associate details.

## Where the Data Comes From

Property records come from public assessor data; people records come from a public directory service. Both draw on information that's already public, not anything Loki Doki collects on its own.

## A Note on Use

This is meant for your own personal reference: figuring out a neighbor, checking an unfamiliar caller, or looking into a property you're curious about. It isn't a substitute for a background-check service and shouldn't be used for anything that requires one (screening a tenant, an employee, or a loan applicant).

## A Note on the Internet

Reverse Lookup needs a connection; both the property and people records are fetched live.
