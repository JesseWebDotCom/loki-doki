Pick the line that sounds like your problem.

## If it stopped answering or pages won't load

1. On the computer that runs MaiPai Home, close the MaiPai window if one is open.
2. Start it again the same way you first installed it (double-click **run**, or run the install command again; it's safe to re-run).
3. Wait about a minute, then open MaiPai in your browser like usual.

Most problems end here. A restart is always safe: your family's data is not
touched.

## If your phone or tablet can't find it

- Make sure the device is on your home Wi-Fi, not cellular data.
- Make sure the computer that runs MaiPai Home is on and awake.
- Open the same address that works on other devices in the house. If you're
  not sure what it is, ask the person who set it up, or check **Settings**
  on a device where MaiPai works.

## If the voice or wake word isn't responding

- Say the wake phrase clearly from a few feet away ("Hey MaiPai", unless
  your family picked another one).
- Check that the device's microphone isn't muted.
- If it still doesn't hear you, restart MaiPai Home (first section above).

## If an answer or picture came out wrong or upsetting

AI makes mistakes. Answers can be wrong, and generated pictures can miss the
mark. Nothing it makes leaves your house, and a parent can tighten any family
member's content settings under **Admin → Family**.

## If it's out of date

Updates are announced on the [releases page](https://github.com/getmaipai/home/releases).
The admin can update from **Admin → Server** with one click.

## Still need help?

Tell us what happened at
[github.com/getmaipai/home/issues](https://github.com/getmaipai/home/issues).
Plain language is fine; you don't need a technical write-up.
